## What's live (use only this)
- **Four games:** Test, ODI, T20I and IPL. Spin six times, draft eleven, play a five-match
  series against a fixed XI (World XI; the All-Star XI in the IPL).
- **IPL:** each spin is a franchise and a block of seasons (2008-12, 2013-17, 2018-22, 2023
  on). A player's card shows only what he did for that team in those seasons. At most four
  overseas players, like the real thing.
- **Pick any XI:** no spins. Search any player, fill the eleven slots, send the link. Your
  friend sees your XI, picks their own, and you both get the same five-match result, slot by
  slot. It keeps a running record against each friend on your phone.
- **Daily Challenge in every format:** same six spins for everyone that day, one scored
  attempt, a streak per format. New spins at 00:00 UTC (5:30 am IST).
- **Challenge a friend from the same spins**, in every format.
- **Result page:** the series, then your XI's strength for batting, bowling and fielding, an
  overall score, a grade (A+ to C) and a tip. Then a share card with both XIs.
- Cards show real career numbers. Individual player ratings are never shown.
- Free. No sign-up, no account.
- Honest limits: streaks and friend records live in your browser only; there is no leaderboard;
  Afghanistan players are missing from the ODI and T20I drafts because the match data isn't
  available.
