## Plan A. IPL: four overseas players

**What we know.** 331 of the 474 draftable IPL players have an ODI or T20I record in our data, so
their country is certain (145 India, 186 overseas). The other 143 have none: mostly Indian domestic
players, but also Afghanistan and associate-nation players (Rashid Khan, Noor Ahmad, Mohammad Nabi,
David Wiese...). Nothing is assumed: the owner marks the overseas ones in
`docs/reports/ipl-overseas-check.md` and the rest are recorded as Indian
(`scripts/cricsheet/owner-overrides.json`).

**The rule.** An IPL XI may hold at most four overseas players. The All-Star XI already has exactly
four (Gayle, de Villiers, Rashid Khan, Malinga).

**Build.**
1. Data: `overseas: true|false` on every IPL player (international record, else the owner's list).
2. Draft rules (`player-logic.ts`): a fifth overseas pick is blocked with the reason "Your XI
   already has four overseas players", exactly like a full role. IPL only.
3. Cards and rows show a small "OS" tag; the dock shows "Overseas 2/4".
4. Squad cut: each 25 keeps enough Indian players per role that a legal XI is always possible.
5. Tests: the "every spin sequence can be drafted into a legal XI" test runs with the cap; the
   calibration is rerun (difficulty will move).

**Questions.**
- A1. Is a player who qualified for another country later (or an associate-nation player) overseas
  if the IPL counted them as overseas? Proposed: follow the IPL's own status; the owner's list decides.
- A2. Show "OS" on overseas players, or mark Indian players instead? Proposed: "OS" on overseas.

## Plan B. IPL: stats for the seasons of the draw

**Today.** A card shows the player's whole IPL career wherever they are drawn. "Pat Cummins, KKR
2018-22" shows all his IPL matches for every team.

**Proposed.** Each card is a *stint*: one player, one franchise, one block of seasons. Its numbers
are only the matches for that team in those seasons, and that is what it is scored on.

**What changes.**
1. Data: the build already counts matches per stint; it would also keep runs, balls, wickets and
   so on per stint (from the ball-by-ball files, so nothing is estimated). File grows about 2.5x.
2. Scoring: percentiles are ranked against all stints, not all careers. The long-career settings
   (60 matches for full credit, 280 for the full bonus) no longer fit, because a five-season block
   holds about 70-80 matches at most; they need new values.
3. Draftable minimum: 10 matches *in the stint* (today: 10 in the career). Squads get smaller; some
   draws may drop under the 5-player minimum and disappear.
4. One player, several cards (Kohli has four RCB stints). An XI can still hold a player only once.
5. All-Star XI: the owner's worry is right, career stats would tower over stint stats. So the
   All-Star XI also becomes eleven *stints*, e.g. "Gayle, RCB 2013-17". The owner picks the stint
   for each of the eleven, or we take each player's best-rated stint.
6. Roles stay per player (from the whole career), so a stint with few innings does not flip a role.
7. Difficulty is recalibrated from scratch; the result page and share card show the stint
   ("RCB 2013-17") next to the name.

**Questions.**
- B1. Minimum matches in a stint to be draftable: 10, or lower (e.g. 7) so squads stay full?
- B2. All-Star XI: keep the same eleven players and use each one's best-rated stint, or do you want
  to choose the stints (or a new eleven)?
- B3. Long-career credit inside a block: proposed full credit at 40 matches in the stint, full
  bonus at 75. These are new numbers, so they need your approval.
- B4. ODI and T20I stay on whole-career stats (their draws are decades, and careers are what fans
  compare). Agreed?

## Plan C. "Pick any XI" mode

**The idea (owner).** No spins. The player searches every player in the game and fills the same
eleven slots (2 openers, 3 middle order, 1 keeper, 2 all-rounder/spinner, 3 fast). They send it to
a friend, who builds their own XI the same way. Both XIs are scored by the engine and both people
see the result.

**How it would work.**
1. New page `/pick` (Test first). Tap a slot, search by name, pick from the matches. Filters for
   role, nation and era. The slot decides the role, with the same rules as the draft (a keeper only
   in slot 6, and so on). Each player once.
2. "Lock in my XI" gives a link, e.g. `/pick?vs=<the XI>`. The XI travels inside the link (the same
   encoding the share links already use), so nothing is stored on a server.
3. The friend opens the link, sees "Beat <name>'s XI" **without seeing the eleven names** (or with,
   see C3), builds theirs, and gets the result: a five-match series between the two XIs on the
   existing result page, with strength bars for both sides.
4. The friend's result link goes back to the first player, so both see the same series.
5. Same engine and series rules as the main game, so the same pair of XIs always gives the same
   result for both people.

**Why it needs thought.**
- With every player available, everyone can pick the same eleven best players. Stats are shown on
  the cards, ratings are not, so the choice is still a judgement, but a "solved" XI will spread.
- No spins means no luck in the squad; the series' small random wobble is the only chance element.

**Questions.**
- C1. Which games first: Test only, or all four?
- C2. Any limit to stop identical "best" XIs? Options: none; at most two players per nation; at
  most N players from the Legends era; or a points budget (each player costs points by rating,
  which would reveal ratings indirectly).
- C3. Does the friend see the first XI before picking? Hidden is fairer (no copying); shown is more
  of a taunt.
- C4. Names: ask each player for a short display name ("Shiva's XI"), or keep it anonymous
  ("Your friend's XI")? A name is typed text inside a link, not stored.
- C5. Should a picked XI also be able to play the World XI, or is this mode only friend v friend?
- C6. Does this mode count toward the anonymous score collection? Proposed: no (it would skew the
  "top X% of drafts" ranking, because these XIs are not drafted).

**Build order once answered.** Search/pick page -> link encoding and the "beat this XI" landing ->
two-XI result (reusing the result page with the friend's XI as the opponent) -> tests -> hidden
preview for the owner -> link from the home page.
